Our Story

Built For
The Community

NOON Burger was born from a simple obsession: make the best halal smash burger the GTA has ever tasted. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just pure flavour.

Every bun is baked in-house using the Tangzhong method — the Japanese technique that gives you that cloud-soft texture with a golden brioche crust. Every patty is smashed to order on a blazing flat griddle, locking in a crust you'll hear across the restaurant.
